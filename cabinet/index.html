The Administration

Who Runs the Departments

The mayor's cabinet, with appointment dates and who each office reports to. Most department heads report to the Chief of Staff, who reports directly to the mayor.

RS
Ron Swanson Mayor
Office of the Mayor · Took office January 2024

Sets the administration's priorities and represents Adversary City to the county and state. Ron Swanson spent eight years on the Ward 4 Council and four running Parks & Public Works before his election.

Read his full biography
TF
Toby Flenderson Chief of Staff
Office of the Mayor · Appointed January 2024

Runs day-to-day operations across all seven departments and manages the executive calendar. Flenderson spent six years in the Law Department before moving to the Mayor's office.

Reports to the Mayor
NC
Niles Crane City Attorney
Law Department · Appointed February 2024

Reviews every ordinance and executive order before the mayor signs it, line by line. Crane has practiced municipal law for eighteen years, the last eleven in Adversary City.

Reports to the Mayor
OM
Oscar Martinez Budget Director
Office of Management & Budget · Appointed January 2024

Builds the annual budget and reports spending against it every quarter. Martinez joined the city's finance division in 2016 and became Budget Director when Mayor Swanson took office.

Reports to the Chief of Staff
LL
Liz Lemon Communications Director
Office of the Mayor · Appointed March 2024

Handles press inquiries and runs the city's public notice channels. Lemon ran communications for a Ward 4 nonprofit before joining the administration.

Reports to the Chief of Staff
AB
Andy Bernard Director of Community Engagement
Office of Neighborhood Affairs · Appointed April 2024

Organizes town halls and ward-level outreach across all seven districts, and keeps the public meetings calendar current.

Reports to the Chief of Staff
