2026 Agenda

Where the Administration Is Focused

Five priorities the mayor's office is tracking this term. Each one below carries its own progress rail and a log of dated updates, pulled from the same record as the Executive Register.

  • Ward Road Resurfacing

    Underway

    Sixty-eight lane-miles repaved since January, funded from the Infrastructure & Public Works capital line. The remaining forty miles are scheduled before winter, weather permitting.

    Recent updates

    • Jul 14, 2026

      Ward 5 repaving finishes two weeks ahead of schedule.

    • Jun 1, 2026

      Ward 3 crews begin resurfacing on Foundry Row.

    • Feb 3, 2026

      EO 2026-04 orders a citywide pothole survey ahead of spring paving.

  • Affordable Housing Trust Fund

    Underway

    Capitalizing a $30 million fund to preserve and build income-restricted units near transit. The fund makes its first developer agreements this year.

    Recent updates

    • Jun 10, 2026

      First developer agreement signed for 40 units near the Ward 2 transit stop.

    • Mar 22, 2026

      Trust fund reaches $10.5 million in committed capital.

    • Jan 20, 2026

      Council approves the trust fund's governing ordinance.

  • Streetlight LED Conversion

    Underway

    Replacing all 11,400 city streetlights with LED fixtures to cut the electricity bill. Conversion crews work ward by ward, starting with the highest-usage corridors.

    Recent updates

    • Jul 1, 2026

      9,234 of 11,400 fixtures converted citywide.

    • Apr 15, 2026

      Ward 6 conversion completes.

    • Jan 9, 2026

      EO 2026-02 exempts the conversion crew from the fiscal year 2026 hiring freeze.

  • Participatory Budgeting Pilot

    Proposed

    Letting residents in three wards vote directly on a slice of next year's capital budget. The council takes up the enabling ordinance this fall.

    Recent updates

    • May 12, 2026

      Council schedules a first reading for September.

    • Feb 28, 2026

      Draft ballot language released for public comment.

  • City Hall Records Digitization

    Completed

    Forty years of paper permits and deeds moved into a searchable archive available in the City Hall reading room.

    Recent updates

    • Dec 18, 2025

      Final batch of permits and deeds scanned; project closed under budget.

    • Aug 4, 2025

      Public reading room terminals go live at City Hall.

    • Jan 2025

      Digitization contract awarded, forty years of records targeted.
