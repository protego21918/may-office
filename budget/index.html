Process

How the budget gets approved.

The Budget Director builds a draft budget each fall, starting from the prior year's actual spending and each department's requested changes. The council holds a public listening session before voting, where residents can speak to specific line items.

The council votes on the final budget in December, and it takes effect at the start of the fiscal year. Any change of more than five percent to a single department's allocation requires a separate ordinance rather than a routine budget amendment.
