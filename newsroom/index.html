News From the Mayor's Office

Press releases and public notices, newest first. For interview requests or comment, contact the Communications Director below.

  • Jul 6, 2026

    Mayor extends heat emergency plan through September

    Cooling centers stay open citywide as the office renews the emergency order under EO 2026-11. Extended hours run at all seven ward community centers through the end of the season.

  • Jun 22, 2026

    Ward Infrastructure Task Force holds first meeting

    Seven ward delegates will set repaving and drainage priorities for fiscal year 2027. The task force meets monthly and reports to the council each quarter.

  • May 30, 2026

    City agencies to post response-time data quarterly

    The new transparency order covers permitting, code enforcement, and 311 requests. The first report is due at the end of the third quarter.

  • Apr 18, 2026

    Mayor's office freezes non-essential travel

    The budget order redirects savings toward the Ward Infrastructure Task Force. Conference and training travel is exempt with department head approval.

  • Mar 3, 2026

    Small Business Permit Fast-Track heads to council

    The mayor's office sends EO 2026-07 to committee for review this month. The program would cut the average storefront permit wait from six weeks to ten days.

  • Feb 21, 2026

    Winter shelter hours extended through March

    EO 2026-06 keeps four warming centers open as cold weather continues, adding overnight capacity at the Ward 3 Community Center.

  • Jan 21, 2026

    Housing Trust Fund ordinance passes council

    The fund starts with $6 million in seed capital from the general fund, with a target of $30 million by the end of the term.

  • Jan 9, 2026

    Mayor's office marks two years in office

    A look back at permit wait times, road miles repaved, and streetlights converted since the administration took office in January 2024.
