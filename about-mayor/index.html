Ron Swanson, 47th Mayor of Adversary City.

Ron Swanson took office in January 2024 after eight years on the Ward 4 City Council and four years running the Department of Parks & Public Works. His platform: fewer permit delays, a repaved road network, and a leaner department budget.

Background

From Ward 4 to City Hall.

Ron Swanson opened a small woodworking and permit-consulting practice in Ward 4 in 2011, working directly with the same permitting office he'd later run. He won a seat on the Ward 4 City Council in 2015 on a promise to cut the average permit wait from six weeks to two, and he kept it inside his first term.

The outgoing mayor appointed him Director of Parks & Public Works in 2019, where he oversaw the department's largest capital budget in a decade and consolidated four maintenance yards into two. Voters elected him the 47th Mayor of Adversary City in November 2023, and he took office that January.

“Give a department a clear budget and a deadline, then get out of its way.”

Mayor Ron Swanson
