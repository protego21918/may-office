2026 Agenda

Where the administration is focused.

Five priorities the mayor's office is tracking this term, updated as they move.

  • Ward Road Resurfacing

    Underway

    Sixty-eight lane-miles repaved since January, with the remaining forty scheduled before winter.

  • Affordable Housing Trust Fund

    Underway

    Capitalizing a $30 million fund to preserve and build income-restricted units near transit.

  • Streetlight LED Conversion

    Underway

    Replacing all 11,400 city streetlights with LED fixtures to cut the electricity bill.

  • Participatory Budgeting Pilot

    Proposed

    Letting residents in three wards vote directly on a slice of next year's capital budget.

  • City Hall Records Digitization

    Completed

    Forty years of paper permits and deeds moved into a searchable public archive.

Inside City Hall

Open sessions, open doors.

Council sessions, budget hearings, and the mayor's weekly office hours all run open to the public, with seating on a first-come basis and an overflow room when the chamber fills. Agendas post at least five days ahead, and every vote is entered into the record the same night.

“A city government that residents can't see into is a city government residents can't hold accountable.”

Mayor Ron Swanson
